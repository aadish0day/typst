### 1.3.1 Purpose of the System

The foundational purpose of **FactStamp** is to **reverse the velocity vector of digital misinformation within dark-social messaging ecosystems**. 

While conventional fact-checking initiatives function primarily as passive, retrospective archival repositories published on external web portals, FactStamp is architected as an **active, counter-propagating intervention** deeply embedded within the communication lifecycle of mobile messaging users. The core purposes of the system encompass:

1. **Weaponizing the Forwarding Mechanism Against Falsehoods:**  
   The primary engine of viral misinformation on WhatsApp is the low-friction, 1-tap forward arrow. FactStamp deliberately leverages this exact behavioral mechanism. Rather than expecting everyday citizens to construct elaborate textual arguments or share external web hyperlinks that are routinely ignored, FactStamp packages verified truth into **authoritative, aesthetically compelling, square ($1080 \times 1080\text{px}$) visual cards**. Because images are consumed instantly within chat threads without leaving the application, users can download and forward the verified PNG stamp back into the originating WhatsApp group in seconds, using the platform's native media-forwarding habits to neutralize rumors at their point of origin.

2. **Dismantling the Centralized Editorial Bottleneck:**  
   Traditional fact-checking newsrooms rely on full-time investigative journalists, creating severe capacity constraints where only a fraction of viral rumors can be analyzed. FactStamp democratizes the verification pipeline by mobilizing an authenticated, distributed corps of **civic volunteers, university students, educators, and domain professionals**. Guided by rigorous mathematical quorum rules ($N \ge 3$) and domain credibility weighting, FactStamp scales horizontally to handle hundreds of hyper-local, regional claims simultaneously without sacrificing factual integrity.

3. **Eliminating Cognitive and Interpersonal Friction:**  
   Correcting an unverified forward sent by a family elder or colleague often produces interpersonal discomfort and defensive reactions. FactStamp depersonalizes corrective discourse. The FactStamp card functions as an **objective, third-party certification stamp**—complete with mathematical confidence ratings and verifiable institutional source links—allowing the sender to post evidence as an impartial public service rather than a personal confrontation.

4. **Sub-Second Duplicate Resolution:**  
   Viral rumors are constantly recycled with minor textual mutations. FactStamp incorporates automated string tokenization and Jaccard similarity metrics ($J \ge 0.75$) to evaluate incoming claims against pre-existing verifications in under 100 milliseconds. When an identical or near-identical forward is submitted, the user is immediately redirected to the certified verdict, preventing redundant verifier effort and delivering instantaneous clarity to the user.

#### Functional Scope Inclusions
- Direct submission of plaintext forwards up to 2,000 characters.
- Drag-and-drop or file-picker upload of JPEG/PNG screenshot images up to 5 MB.
- Client-side Canvas API image resizing and compression keeping base64 payloads under 500 KB, stored directly in Firestore under a zero cloud storage bill model.
- Tokenization, stop-word filtering, and Jaccard similarity matching ($J \ge 0.75$).
- Public verification queue with real-time Firestore v12.17.0 WebSocket subscriptions.
- Three-verifier quorum gating ($N \ge 3$) and multi-factor consensus confidence calculation ($C = 0.40 A + 0.30 R + 0.30 S$).
- Client-side compilation and one-click download of 1080×1080px square PNG fact cards via `html-to-image` 1.11.13.
- Interactive analytics dashboard detailing weekly misinformation categories and top verifier leaderboards via Recharts 2.10.0.

#### Explicit Exclusions & Delimitations
- **No Native App Dependency:** FactStamp does not require native Android or iOS app installation; it functions universally across any modern mobile or desktop web browser.
- **No Scraping of Private WhatsApp Chats:** FactStamp respects personal privacy and legal encryption standards; it never passively reads chat logs or accesses private contact lists. All verification requests are exclusively user-initiated.
- **No Unchecked Automated AI Hallucinations:** Unlike generative AI chat bots that frequently hallucinate factual evidence, FactStamp mandates verifiable human review coupled with primary institutional citations (e.g., government gazettes, WHO bulletins, judicial orders).

### 1.3.3 Applicability Across Stakeholders

FactStamp is designed for broad real-world applicability across distinct societal and institutional domains:

1. **General WhatsApp Recipients (Everyday Citizens):**  
   Individuals receiving suspicious forwards regarding miracle health cures, government welfare schemes, banking scams, or exam cancellations can verify claims in seconds before circulating them to friends and family.

2. **Student Fact-Checkers & Civic Volunteers:**  
   Undergraduate and postgraduate IT, journalism, and humanities students can apply digital literacy skills to research primary sources, submit certified verifications, and earn verifiable platform reputation scores.

3. **Family & Society WhatsApp Group Administrators:**  
   Group administrators often struggle to maintain harmony when inflammatory or false rumors circulate. FactStamp delivers indisputable, color-coded visual stamps that admins can post to authoritatively settle group disputes without appearing biased.

4. **Academic Researchers & Fact-Checking Organizations:**  
   Social scientists, media researchers, and professional fact-checking organizations can monitor FactStamp's aggregated public analytics to detect emerging rumor patterns, seasonal hoax categories, and misinformation propagation velocity across Indian dark social networks.
