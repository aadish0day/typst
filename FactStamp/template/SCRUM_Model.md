# Terms:
Product Backlog: The Product Backlog is the complete list of features, enhancements, bug fixes, and improvements required for the product. It is prioritized and continuously updated.
Sprint Backlog: The Sprint Backlog is the subset of Product Backlog items selected for the current sprint.
Daily Scrum (Daily Stand-up): A Daily Scrum is a 15-minute meeting held every day where the development team synchronizes work and discusses progress.
Scrum Master: The Scrum Master ensures that the Scrum process is followed and removes obstacles that slow down the team.The Scrum Master is not the team's boss or project manager. Instead, they act as a facilitator and coach.
Product Owner: The Product Owner represents the customer and is responsible for maximizing the value of the product.

# SCRUM Model
It is a popular Agile Methodology.
Scrum is a lightweight, iterative and incremental framework.
Scrum breaks down the development phases into stages or cycles called “sprints”.
The development time for each sprint is maximized and dedicated, thereby managing only one sprint at a time.
Scrum Team has scrum master, product owner with constant communications on the daily basis.
Terms: Backlog, Sprint, Daily Scrum, Scrum Master, Product owner.

# Advantages:
 High-quality, low-risk product.
 Reduce the development time up to 40%
 Scrum customer satisfaction is very important.
 Reviewing the current sprint before moving to new one.

# Disadvantages:
More efficient for small team size.
 No changes in the sprint.
